Blocked Channels

Paste a channel name, an @handle, a channel ID (UC…) or a channel URL. Matching ignores case, accents, spaces and a leading @, and the whole name must match. A channel ID or a name that differs from its handle is linked automatically once you open that channel's page or one of its videos.

    Blocked Keywords & Hashtags

    Keywords match anywhere inside a video title, ignoring case, accents and extra spaces. Anything starting with # is added as a hashtag.

    Keywords

      Hashtags

        Hashtags in titles, Shorts captions, search snippets and visible hashtag links are blocked everywhere. Hashtags that appear only in a video's description are caught when the video is opened, then remembered and hidden from all lists afterward.

        A hashtag matches the whole tag: #gaming does not match #gamingsetup or the plain word "gaming".

        Turns on every blocking feature — Shorts, home feed, Up Next, comments, autoplay, Explore/Trending, and channel, keyword & hashtag blocking — for the duration you set, then puts every switch back the way it was.

        Backup

        YouTube Focus Mode helps you use YouTube intentionally by putting its most distracting design patterns behind switches you control. Every block is optional, reversible, and local — nothing is sent anywhere.

        To catch Shorts and posts that show no channel or hashtag in the feed, the extension may look up public metadata for those items on youtube.com itself. Nothing is sent anywhere else.
